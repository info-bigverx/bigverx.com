03 — About

About BIGVERX

BIGVERX Consultancy is a visa and immigration consultancy providing consultation and application assistance to individuals, students and businesses seeking international travel opportunities.

Our work centres on the practical side of applying for a visa — understanding the requirements that apply to your purpose of travel, preparing your documentation carefully, and assembling a complete, well-presented application for submission. We guide you through each stage in clear language, so you know what is required and what to expect next.

Whether you are planning a family visit, preparing to study abroad, or travelling for business, our role is to make the process organised, transparent and easier to manage.

  • Honest guidance on visa categories, eligibility and requirements
  • Careful organisation and review of supporting documentation
  • Clear communication from first consultation to final submission

Work Visa Procedure Assistance

BIGVERX can provide guidance regarding work visa procedures, documentation and required application steps.

This is a procedure assistance service. A work visa is not one of the three main visa categories offered by BIGVERX — Visit, Study and Business visa remain the consultancy's primary services.
Assistance covers
  • 01Understanding the general work visa procedure and how the process is structured.
  • 02Documentation requirements and preparing an organised document set.
  • 03The required application steps and the sequence in which they are completed.
  • 04General guidance on what to prepare before approaching the relevant authority.
